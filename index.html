Our Story

Al Nisaa Islamic Institution was founded in the year 2014,by a a group of Women lead by Usthada Farhiya Abdirizack Garane with the help of Garissa county government.
The Jamaca started the Institution with one branch at Garissa county. The branch was the first time launched on 15th july 2014 by the first governor of Garissa county his excellency Nathif Jamaa.

Our mission

After the launch,the Institution took off to suport, empower and build women in the study of Islamic religion, culture and morals.
Today the Institution has three branches in three different counties.Our main mision is to encourage women,boost their Islamic Knowledge,help them grow and train them to be the future leaders in the society.

Quran Memorization

We Offer the learning of the Quran for beginners and Memorization for the who finished the quran once or even twise.
The Quran will be tought by female teachers who are dedicated in teaching their students and have more than 10 years of experence.
